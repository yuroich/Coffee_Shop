Our story

Brewed & Blessed started in 2019 as a tiny corner stall with a secondhand espresso machine and a stubborn belief that good coffee shouldn't be expensive or complicated.

Today we serve our neighborhood from the same corner — just with better equipment at the same honest prices. Every drink is made by hand, every bean is sourced locally, and every cup goes out with a little extra care.

We're not a chain. We're not trying to be. We just love what we do, and we hope it shows in every sip.
